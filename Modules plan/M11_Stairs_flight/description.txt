# M11 — Stairs — Flight + Parapet

**Dimensions:** 10 steps: 1.25 W x 0.30 D x 0.15 R (rise 1.5 m total), parapet 0.5 m high

## What the module is
Straight stone stair flight connecting two levels (hilly towns). One side: low stone parapet; optional variant: green iron railing (thin bars). Steps are a single repeated module (one step mesh x 10).

## Materials
Stone (steps, parapet), green iron (railing variant), optional greenery (vine on parapet — low-poly leaves).
