# M10 — Balcony — Iron Railing

**Dimensions:** slab 1.25 x 0.6 x 0.15 m, railing height 0.9 m, bar spacing 0.12 m

## What the module is
Small facade balcony: projecting stone slab with a wrought-iron railing (thin vertical bars + top rail). Second variant: stone balustrade (short balusters) — same footprint.

## Materials
Stone (slab, balustrade variant), black iron (bars, top rail).
