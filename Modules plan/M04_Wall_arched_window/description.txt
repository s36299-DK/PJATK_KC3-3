# M04 — Wall — Arched Window

**Dimensions:** 2.5 x 2.5 x 0.3 m, opening 0.9 x 1.4 m, arch radius 0.45 m

## What the module is
Facade panel with a window that has a semicircular arched top. Wooden shutters continue under the arch (arched-top shutter variant).

## Materials
Stucco, painted wood (frame + arched shutters), glass.
