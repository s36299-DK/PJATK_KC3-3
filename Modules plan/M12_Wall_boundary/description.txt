# M12 — Wall — Low Boundary

**Dimensions:** 2.5 x 0.5 x 0.3 m, stone cap 0.35 m wide, end post 0.5 x 0.7 x 0.35 m

## What the module is
Garden/property boundary wall, 0.5 m high: stone plinth with stucco upper part and a stone cap; end posts slightly taller. Repeats horizontally to fence a yard; one variant with a 1 m gap for a gate.

## Materials
Stone (plinth, cap, posts), stucco (upper part).
