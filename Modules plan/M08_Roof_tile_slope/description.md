# M08 — Roof — Tile Slope Module

**Dimensions:** 2.5 x 2.5 m slope plane, pitch 30 deg, ridge cap 0.15 x 0.15 m

## What the module is
One roof slope: terracotta coppi (curved half-tiles) in 4-5 rows with overlapping edges, ridge cap along the top edge, eaves overhang 0.15 m. Modules join along the 2.5 m sides to extend the roof.

## Materials
Terracotta (tiles, ridge, eaves).
