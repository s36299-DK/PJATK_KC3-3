# M14 — Rooftop — Terrace

**Dimensions:** slab 2.5 x 2.5 m, railing 0.9 m high on 3 sides, door opening 1.0 x 2.1 m

## What the module is
Flat roof terrace (Ligurian style): slab with a 0.15 m lip, iron railing around three sides, and an optional small table with two chairs set. Accessed from a roof door in the gable wall.

## Materials
Stone/concrete (slab), black iron (railing), wood (table, chairs).
