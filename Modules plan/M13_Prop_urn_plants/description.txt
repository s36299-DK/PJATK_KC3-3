# M13 — Props — Urn + Plants

**Dimensions:** urn dia 0.35 x 0.45 h; pot dia 0.3 x 0.3 h; cactus 0.5 h

## What the module is
Set of small props that give the facade life: terracotta urn on a pedestal, potted plant (simple leaf clusters), cactus. Placed on sills, terraces, ground.

## Materials
Terracotta (urn, pots), green (leaves, cactus).
