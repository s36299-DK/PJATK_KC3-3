# M05 — Wall — Corner

**Dimensions:** 2.5 x 2.5 m footprint, two 0.3 m planes at 90 degrees, stone corner trim 0.3 m wide

## What the module is
L-shaped corner module joining two facade planes. Stone corner trim (vertical strip of stone blocks) protects the corner — visible in most references.

## Materials
Stucco (planes), stone (corner trim, plinth at the base 0.5 m high).
