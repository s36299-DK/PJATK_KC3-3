# M02 — Wall — Window + Shutters

**Dimensions:** 2.5 x 2.5 x 0.3 m, window 0.9 x 1.2 m (sill 0.9 m above floor)

## What the module is
Facade panel with one rectangular window: white wooden frame, stone lintel above the opening, and a pair of wooden louvered shutters. Model two states of the shutters: closed and open (rotated against the wall).

## Materials
Stucco (wall), stone (lintel), painted wood (frame, shutters: white / sage green / sky blue), glass.
