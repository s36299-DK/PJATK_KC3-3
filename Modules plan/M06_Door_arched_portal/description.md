# M06 — Door — Arched Stone Portal

**Dimensions:** portal 1.6 x 2.7 m, door 1.0 x 2.1 m, stone step 1.0 x 0.3 x 0.15 m

## What the module is
Ground-floor entrance: arched stone portal surrounding a wooden double door. The portal is a separate stone frame (heavier than the wall), with a stone threshold step in front.

## Materials
Stone (portal arch, step), painted wood (double door, weathered green/brown), stucco (wall plane behind).
