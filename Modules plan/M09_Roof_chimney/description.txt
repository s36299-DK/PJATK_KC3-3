# M09 — Roof — Chimney

**Dimensions:** 0.5 x 0.5 m base, 1.5 m high, cap 0.6 x 0.6 x 0.1 m

## What the module is
Square chimney stack sitting on the roof slope: brick/terracotta body, slightly wider stone cap, one terracotta flue pot on top.
